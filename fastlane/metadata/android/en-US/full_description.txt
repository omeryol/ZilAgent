ZilAgent helps teachers track school bell schedules quickly and clearly.

Key features:
- Fully offline architecture (no internet permission).
- Profile-based schedules (e.g. normal day, exam day, weekend course).
- Two home screen widgets:
  - Live Countdown (remaining time + current phase)
  - Lesson Flow (today's remaining lessons/breaks)
- Exam mode with large fullscreen countdown for classroom visibility.
- Local backup/restore and QR profile transfer.
- Turkish and English language support.

ZilAgent focuses on practical classroom timing and privacy-friendly local data.
